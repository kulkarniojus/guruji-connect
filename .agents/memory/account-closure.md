---
name: Account closure
description: User-initiated account closure behavior and related record-retention safeguards.
---

Account closure immediately blocks sign-in and invalidates existing sessions. Guruji availability must be turned off so the profile cannot receive new bookings. Keep linked booking, event, payment, refund, and accounting history so service and financial records remain usable. Never allow the last active admin or Super Admin to close their account. Notify remaining admins to review any obligations connected to the closed account.

**Why:** the user asked that customers and Gurujis cannot log in again after closing their account, while existing operations and financial history must remain intact.

**How to apply:** use a password-confirmed self-service action, enforce account protections on the server, and retain business records rather than deleting rows that bookings or refunds reference.

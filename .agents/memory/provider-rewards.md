---
name: Provider rewards
description: Eligibility and fulfillment policy for Guruji and Other-category provider offers.
---

Provider reward offers may target Gurujis, approved active Other-category providers, or both. Other-category providers keep the customer account role; provider access depends on an approved active category application. Eligibility uses completed services and customer-to-provider ratings within the configured rolling window. Deactivation must not erase rewards already earned. Reward fulfillment is an admin-confirmed record only; the system must not transfer bonus money or purchase/send vouchers automatically. Keep actual monetary reward fulfillment restricted to Super Admin.

**Why:** The user wants configurable incentives for provider quality and completed pujas while preserving the existing Other-category account model and Super Admin financial controls.

**How to apply:** Keep offers private to approved providers, make criteria and progress explicit, persist earned eligibility, and require Super Admin to record bonus, voucher, or gift delivery.

---
name: Puja commission policy
description: Commission basis, individual overrides, and immutable booking allocations.
---

Commission is a split of the final customer booking total after discounts, adjustments and selected samagri, not an additional customer charge.

**Why:** The user specified commission on the exact customer booking price after discounts, rather than the catalogue base price.

**How to apply:** Calculate portal and Guruji shares from the same authoritative final total used to create the booking; round to paise and keep the two shares equal to that total.

Individual puja percentages override the default. Ordinary default changes preserve individual exceptions; applying one rate uniformly to all pujas explicitly removes overrides and needs a clear confirmation.

**Why:** The user needs both all-puja configuration and individual control. A routine default change must not silently erase individual configuration.

**How to apply:** New bookings retain a commission snapshot that later configuration changes must not rewrite. Do not invent a starting percentage from the user's 20%/30% examples, or backfill historical bookings using today's policy. Allocations are records, not proof of payment or payout.

Guruji must see the saved commission payable to Vaidik Dhara and their remaining share before accepting, and explicitly acknowledge positive commission.

**Why:** The user said Guruji was unaware of commission and asked for the commission obligation and expected share to be explained when accepting a puja.

**How to apply:** Disclose the booking's saved percentage rather than hardcoding 20%; record the acknowledgement together with genuine acceptance. Unknown historical allocations must stay unknown, not be silently priced using current rules.
